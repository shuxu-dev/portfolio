Research

I study how sensory health and the environments people live in shape mobility in later life.

Driving and vision loss

How is visual impairment related to whether older adults drive and which situations they avoid? This study examines driving status and avoidance among older adults in the United States.

Read the driving study

Vision, home hazards, and falls

How do visual function and hazards at home relate to falls? This study considers vision and the home environment together.

Sensory impairment and homebound status

What different patterns of being homebound can we identify among older adults with sensory impairment? This project received support from the 2026 A. Regula Herzog Young Investigators Fund.
