About me

Hi, I’m Shu. I’m a gerontologist and Research Investigator at University of Michigan Institute for Social Research, Survey Research Center.

How does sensory loss change where older adults can go and what they can do? I study how vision and hearing loss relate to physical health, daily functioning, and well-being, and how people’s surroundings shape their mobility, independence, and social participation.
