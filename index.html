About me

Hi, I’m Shu. I’m a Research Investigator at the University of Michigan’s Survey Research Center, part of the Institute for Social Research.

How does vision loss change where older adults can go? What makes a home easier or harder to navigate? I study how vision and hearing loss shape mobility and participation in later life, and how the places people live affect those experiences.

I draw on population-based aging studies to examine everyday activities such as driving and getting around at home.
